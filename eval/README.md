# Evaluation data

`questions.jsonl` is generated by `build_dataset.py` from the hand-authored lists in `authored/`. Each answerable question names a verbatim phrase from the clause that answers it; the script widens the phrase to the full sentence and stores that sentence as the gold evidence, failing loudly if the phrase is not found in the source text.

**Provenance.** The questions were drafted by an AI assistant from the clause text, and **have not yet been reviewed by the author**. Before quoting any number from them as the author's own work, the author should read at least a sample of questions and gold sentences and correct them. The unanswerable questions were checked by looking at the top retrieved passages for each, not by an exhaustive search of the corpus. The Hindi and Hinglish questions were written as paraphrases of answerable ones and have not been checked by a native speaker.

**Split.** 50/50 by hash of the question id (paraphrases follow their source). All thresholds and design choices were made on `dev`. `test` was run once for the final configuration.

**Known limits.** 148 questions; 22 unanswerable on test (16 English, 6 Hinglish, 4 Hindi, the latter two translated from English ones and inheriting their split); the English test split was looked at twice (once under the dense rule, once under the reranker rule; no threshold was fitted on it); one corpus; gold is one sentence per evidence item, so a correct answer drawn from a neighbouring sentence of the same clause can count as a miss.
